# Student Satisfaction About Cafeteria – NIBM

##  Project Overview

**Student Satisfaction About Cafeteria – NIBM** is a first-year final project conducted to investigate the level of student satisfaction with the cafeteria facilities and services at **NIBM – NIC Campus**.

The project was based on **original survey data collected directly from students**. The collected responses were analyzed using statistical techniques in **R**, with the analysis and findings documented using **R Markdown**. The study focused on understanding students' opinions and experiences regarding important aspects of the cafeteria, such as food quality, pricing, cleanliness, service quality, variety, availability, and overall satisfaction.

The analysis was used to identify common satisfaction levels, areas that require improvement, and factors that may influence students' overall cafeteria experience. Based on the findings, practical recommendations were proposed to help improve cafeteria services and enhance the overall student experience on campus.

##  Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

* Measure the overall satisfaction level of students with the NIBM cafeteria.
* Identify the main factors affecting student satisfaction.
* Analyze student opinions regarding food quality, pricing, cleanliness, service, and other cafeteria facilities.
* Identify areas where students experienced dissatisfaction.
* Use statistical analysis to interpret the collected survey data.
* Provide practical recommendations for improving cafeteria services.

##  Data Collection

The dataset used in this project was collected through an **original student survey** conducted among students at NIBM – NIC Campus.

The survey collected responses related to students' experiences and opinions about the cafeteria. The responses were organized and prepared for statistical analysis before being imported into R.

The project therefore demonstrates the complete process from **primary data collection → data preparation → statistical analysis → visualization → interpretation → recommendations**.

##  Methodology

The project followed a structured data analysis process:

1. **Survey Design**
   A questionnaire was prepared to collect students' opinions about different aspects of the cafeteria.

2. **Data Collection**
   Responses were collected directly from NIBM – NIC students.

3. **Data Preparation**
   The collected data was organized and prepared for analysis, including checking the dataset and handling the data in a structured format.

4. **Exploratory Data Analysis**
   The dataset was explored to understand response distributions and identify important patterns.

5. **Statistical Analysis**
   Statistical techniques were applied using R to analyze student satisfaction and relationships between relevant variables.

6. **Data Visualization**
   Charts and graphical representations were created to communicate the results clearly.

7. **Interpretation**
   The statistical results were interpreted in the context of student cafeteria experiences.

8. **Recommendations**
   Based on the findings, recommendations were developed to identify possible improvements to cafeteria services.

##  Key Areas Analyzed

The study considered different aspects of the cafeteria experience, including:

*  Food quality
*  Food pricing and affordability
*  Cleanliness and hygiene
*  Service quality
*  Food and menu variety
*  Service efficiency
*  Cafeteria facilities
*  Overall student satisfaction
